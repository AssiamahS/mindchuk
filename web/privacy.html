Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The short version

MindChuk is a notes app that keeps everything on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. We never see your notes.

1. Who we are

MindChuk is published by Sylvester Assiamah ("we"). You can reach us through the project's GitHub issues at github.com/AssiamahS/mindchuk/issues.

2. Information we collect

None. MindChuk does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our systems. There is no sign-up, no email, no phone number, and no tracking of any kind.

3. Where your notes live

Notes, tags, reminders, and any photos you attach are stored in the app's private container on your device using Apple's on-device database. They are included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own iOS settings. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it.

4. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Notifications: to deliver reminders you create with "remind me". Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Photos: only when you choose to attach a photo to a note. The selected photo is copied into the note; the app does not scan your library.

5. Links you save

If you tap a link inside a note, it opens in your browser. Nothing is fetched or previewed in the background.

6. Export

You can export all of your notes as a CSV file from Settings at any time and share it wherever you like. Where that file goes is entirely up to you.

7. Children

MindChuk does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue.
